    Limit and rules

    200 is the protected-consumer limit in NEPRA's tariff as of October 2026. Change it if the rule changes.

    How it decides

    1. Two readings inLast bill's reading and today's, typed or read from a photo. The photo reader refuses rather than guesses.
    2. A range, not a numberA model trained on Lahore homes gives a likely total and the range 8 in 10 months like this one end in.
    3. Plain code speaksSimple rules, not a model, decide whether to say anything. If it does, you get a daily budget and what it assumed.

    The forecast is in units because units are what you control. Rupee figures are estimates on the February 2026 tariff plus this month's fuel adjustment, and leave out lifeline and income tax. This is not a bill.
